Rough cuts from huge footage

My livestream clipper. It finds every moment I'm on camera in someone else's 24-hour stream and cuts each one into a clip.

  1. Learns my face from clips I've already posted.
  2. Checks the stream about once a second and scores every face against mine.
  3. Groups the matches into appearances and cuts each one with a few seconds of padding.
  4. Ranks the best moments by how fast chat was moving, so I edit those first.

Talking head to rough cut

Turns a raw talking-head recording into a rough cut with graphics already placed. That's how the panel cut and my course videos started.

  1. Transcribes the recording word by word with Whisper.
  2. Trims long pauses and dead air automatically.
  3. Cuts me out of the background so graphics can sit behind or beside me.
  4. Places each card on the exact word it belongs to, then I tune the timing by hand.

About

I'm a student at Cal State Fullerton who edits for my own TikTok and for a course I made. I care about pacing most: cutting until nothing is left that the viewer would skip. I like working from rough ideas, showing early cuts and turning feedback around fast.

I've edited since middle school, when I posted 45 gaming videos to YouTube, cut in Premiere Pro and After Effects with thumbnails made in Photoshop. Today I also use CapCut, and I'm happiest when part of the job can be turned into a script.
