Panel to vertical: three shorts from a one-hour talk

A one-hour panel on AI and security, shot on one wide, static camera. I cut it into three vertical shorts, each built to hold attention from the first second.

Panel to vertical

I reframed each speaker to a close-up and cut them out of the background.

A picture on every point

Cards pop in on the word they illustrate, so there is always something new on screen.

Headline hook

A two-line title holds the opening seconds, then hands off to small, clean captions.
